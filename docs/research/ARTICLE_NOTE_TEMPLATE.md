# Article review template

[Research index](README.md) · [Evaluation methodology](METHODOLOGY.md)

Copy this structure for a substantive follow-up review. Replace all placeholders
before claiming completion; do not count a blank template as reviewed evidence.

## Source

- Stable research ID:
- Title and authors:
- Journal/venue and first online date:
- Volume year, revision/version and errata checked:
- DOI and primary-source URL:
- Review date and access depth:
- Paper/code/data license and redistribution decision:

## Published result

Summarize the claim, comparator, assumptions, supported domain and limitations in
original words. Identify exact sections/equations when they have been reviewed.

## Project hypothesis

- Owning issue and baseline SHA:
- Current kernel/API and affected callers:
- Proposed benefit and falsifiable acceptance criterion:
- Alternatives and reasons not to adopt:
- Compatibility, parameterization and scope decisions:

## Experiment protocol

- Frozen preregistration and expected movement:
- Exact binary64 inputs and independent reference algorithms:
- Precision/convergence checks and unresolved oracle gaps:
- Fitting grid and untouched holdout:
- Metrics, seams, endpoints and negative controls:
- Runtime/cost experiment:
- Exact-source Excel evidence requirements:

## Results and decision

- Evidence paths, candidate SHA and reproduction commands:
- Observed results and all failures:
- Unmeasured or unsupported domains:
- Decision: adopt / defer / reject, with rationale:
- Follow-up issue, owner and target date:

Keep publication claims, model predictions and Excel observations separate.
