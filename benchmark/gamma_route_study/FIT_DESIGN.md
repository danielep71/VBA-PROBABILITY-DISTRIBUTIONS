# #307 repaired binary64 route: fit set design (2026-11-10)

Frozen before the Excel export that fills it. The inputs are in
`fit_design.py`. Like the map, this is a **fitting set**: the routing rule is
derived here and then tested on the untouched holdout of
[`HOLDOUT_DESIGN.md`](HOLDOUT_DESIGN.md).

## Why

The map ([`MAP_RESULT.md`](MAP_RESULT.md)) located the errors of the pre-#242
binary64 route: the prefactor's deviance, the continued fraction's stop at
`PROB_NUM_EPS`, and the complement `Q = 1 - P`. A Python study then found two
more, in the lower series: it stopped while the remaining terms still summed
to several ulps, and its plain sum lost up to 1E-14 at large shapes.

The repaired route fixes all of them, in binary64 and at about binary64 cost:

- **Factor.** For `A > 1`, `x^A e^-x / Gamma(A) = sqrt(A / (2 pi)) exp(-(D + stirlerr(A)))`.
  Loader's deviance `D` is an unevaluated pair from error-free transformations, accurate to
  about 1E-17 absolute, and the exponent stays a pair. For `A <= 1`, the small-shape factor
  the continued fraction already uses.
- **Series.** A compensated sum, stopped only when the remaining terms sum to at most half
  an ulp.
- **Continued fraction.** Stopped at machine epsilon at every shape.

`route_model.py` models it statement by statement, with VBA's 64-bit
intermediates. Over the map's 282 points it meets the governing contract on
every tail it returns. The VBA is generated by `b64_route_vba.py` into
`M_STATS_PROBDIST_SPECIALFUNCS.bas` as `PROB_TryGammaB64SeriesP` and
`PROB_TryGammaB64ContinuedFractionQ`. Nothing routes to it yet.

## Inputs

- **The map's 282 points** (`design.py`).
- **Eight more shapes for the cost:** 2E3, 3E3, 5E3, 2E4, 3E4, 5E4, 2E5 and 5E5, at the map's offsets
  `X = A + k sqrt(A)` and the seam points: 104 points.
- **Six edge cases:**
  - `X / A` subnormal, where the route declines;
  - the factor's floor at `A > 1` and at `A <= 1`;
  - a shape of 1E-8;
  - a tiny but normal `X / A`;
  - a point just below the small-shape floor.
- **Total:** 392 points. None is a holdout shape or its doubling (`check_disjoint.py`).

## What Excel records

At every point the exporter `M_STATS_PROBDIST_GBF.bas` records:

- the status and value of both repaired routines;
- the echoed inputs;
- the mean microseconds per call of the routine that answered. The count doubles from 16
  until at least 0.2 s has elapsed, the method of the #298 benchmark.

## How it is scored

`analyze_b64_fit.py`:

1. **Fidelity.** At every point, each routine's status and value must equal `route_model.py`
   bit for bit. That includes a decline wherever the model declines. Any mismatch stops the
   study until it is explained.
2. **Accuracy.** This part is exploratory. Each tail the rule would return is scored against
   its governing contract, as in the map:
   - below `A + 1`, P from the series, and `Q = 1 - P` only where `P <= 0.5`; otherwise Q
     stays with the double-double kernel, the #24 principle;
   - from `A + 1`, Q from the continued fraction and `P = 1 - Q`, where `Q < 0.5`.
3. **Cost.** For each routine, it fits `c0 + c1 n` microseconds per call against the
   iteration count `n` of the model.

## The shape ceiling A2 (frozen rule)

The cost of the series grows like the square root of the shape. Rule H6 of
the holdout requires every binary64-routed call to cost under 0.2 ms. So the
rule routes to binary64 only up to a shape ceiling A2, fixed here in advance.
The budget is **0.1 ms**, half of H6's limit, to absorb run-to-run variation.

**A2** is the largest value of the ladder

`1E3, 2E3, 3E3, 5E3, 1E4, 2E4, 3E4, 5E4, 1E5, 2E5, 5E5, 1E6`

for which:

- every fit point with shape at most A2 measured at most 0.1 ms; and
- the fitted cost model predicts at most 0.1 ms at A2's worst arguments: the series at the
  predecessor of `A2 + 1`, and the continued fraction at `A2 + 1`.

The ladder stops at the first value that fails either condition.

## Use

After the fit:

- If fidelity holds, the routing rule is preregistered with this A2, with its predictions
  for the holdout, before the holdout is evaluated.
- If fidelity fails, the model or the port is corrected and the fit is run again.

No contract or threshold changes here, and no public function changes.
